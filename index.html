Process (03)

A process built to remove surprises

You always know what's being built this week, what it costs, and what's left. Every engagement runs the same four stages.

01

Scope & shape

1–2 weeks

A paid discovery sprint where we pin the problem down: user flows, technical constraints, the smallest version worth shipping. You leave with a spec, an architecture and a fixed quote — whether or not you build it with us.

02

Design in the open

2–3 weeks

Flows and interface design reviewed with you twice a week. We design against real data and real edge cases, not three happy-path screens.

03

Build in two-week slices

6–16 weeks

Working software in a staging environment from week one. Every fortnight: a demo, a deployed build you can click through, and an honest burn-down of what's left.

04

Launch & hand over

Ongoing

Release, monitoring, documentation and a walkthrough for your team. Then we step back, or stay on retainer. Your code, your repos, your accounts — always.

Questions (05)

The things clients ask first

01 What does a project typically cost?

Most first releases land between $25k and $90k depending on scope. Discovery sprints start at $4k and are credited against the build if you continue with us. We'll give you a number before you commit to anything — we don't do open-ended hourly billing unless you specifically want it.

02 How fast can you start?

Usually within two to three weeks. Discovery can often begin sooner, since it runs with a smaller team.

03 Who owns the code and the accounts?

You do, from day one. We build in your GitHub organisation and your cloud accounts wherever possible, so there's never a handover hostage situation.

04 Do you work with non-technical founders?

Often. In that case we act as your technical partner — making architecture calls, explaining trade-offs in plain language, and flagging the decisions that are genuinely yours to make.

05 Can you take over an existing codebase?

Yes. We start with a paid audit: what's solid, what's risky, what it would cost to fix versus replace. You get that assessment in writing whether or not you hire us for the work.
