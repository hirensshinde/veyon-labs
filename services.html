Services Six disciplines

Everything between the idea and the release

We take on complete builds rather than staffing seats. That means one team, one plan, and one person accountable for whether the thing actually works.

01

Web development

From a marketing site that loads instantly to a multi-tenant SaaS platform with role-based access and billing. We write TypeScript end to end and test the parts that would hurt if they broke.

Included
  • Next.js / React front-ends
  • Node.js and serverless APIs
  • Postgres data modelling
  • Auth, roles and permissions
  • Stripe and payments
  • Headless CMS (Sanity, Payload)
You get
  • Production app in your repos
  • Automated test suite and CI
  • Staging and production environments
  • Architecture and runbook docs
  • Lighthouse 95+ on core pages
02

Mobile app development

Cross-platform by default, because two codebases usually costs twice for no benefit. When you genuinely need native — heavy graphics, deep OS integration, specialised hardware — we say so up front.

Included
  • React Native and Flutter
  • Offline-first storage and sync
  • Push notifications and deep links
  • Biometrics and secure storage
  • In-app purchases
  • Crash reporting and analytics
You get
  • iOS and Android apps in the stores
  • Store listings and release pipeline
  • Over-the-air update setup
  • Device-matrix QA report
  • Handover session for your team
03

Product strategy & design

The cheapest bug to fix is the feature you decided not to build. Discovery is where we work out what the product needs to do, who it's for, and what can wait until version two.

Included
  • Stakeholder and user interviews
  • Journey mapping and flows
  • Clickable prototypes
  • Interface design in Figma
  • Design system and components
  • Accessibility review (WCAG 2.2 AA)
You get
  • A written, buildable specification
  • Fixed-price quote for the build
  • Figma file you own outright
  • Prioritised roadmap for v1 and v2
04

Cloud & DevOps

Infrastructure sized for the traffic you have, with a clear path to the traffic you want. We favour boring, well-understood tools over anything that needs a specialist to keep alive.

Included
  • AWS, Vercel and Cloudflare
  • Infrastructure as code (Terraform)
  • CI/CD with GitHub Actions
  • Monitoring, logging and alerting
  • Backups and disaster recovery
  • Cost review and rightsizing
You get
  • Reproducible infrastructure in code
  • One-command deploys
  • On-call runbook
  • Monthly cost forecast
05

AI integration

LLM features that earn their place: document search that finds the right clause, support triage that routes correctly, extraction that beats manual entry. With evaluation, so you know when it regresses.

Included
  • RAG pipelines and vector search
  • Agentic workflows and tool use
  • Prompt evaluation harnesses
  • Streaming UX patterns
  • Guardrails and PII handling
  • Token cost monitoring
You get
  • A measurably accurate feature
  • Eval suite you can run in CI
  • Cost-per-request breakdown
  • Fallback behaviour when the model fails
06

Audit, rescue & rebuild

For teams who inherited something fragile. We go through the codebase, infrastructure and delivery process, then give you a prioritised plan — including the parts that are fine and don't need touching.

Included
  • Codebase and architecture review
  • Security and dependency audit
  • Performance profiling
  • Test coverage assessment
  • Incremental migration planning
  • Team process review
You get
  • Written findings with severity ratings
  • Costed remediation plan
  • Quick wins delivered in week one
  • Optional ongoing engineering support
Engagements (02)

Three ways to work with us

Every engagement starts with a conversation and a written scope. No proposals full of filler, no surprise change orders.

01 — Discovery from $4k

Discovery sprint

One to two weeks to turn an idea into a spec, an architecture and a fixed quote. Credited against the build if you continue.

  • Best for validating before committing
  • Output: spec, designs, fixed price
02 — Most common from $25k

Fixed-scope build

A defined product, a defined price, a defined date. We absorb the estimation risk — that's what discovery is for.

  • Best for a v1 or a major new module
  • Output: shipped, supported product
03 — Ongoing from $9k / mo

Embedded team

A senior pod working alongside your people on a rolling monthly basis. Cancel with 30 days' notice, no lock-in.

  • Best for continuous product work
  • Output: predictable delivery cadence
